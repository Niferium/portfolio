About

A software engineer moving deeper into applied AI.

I'm Harley Davidson Tobias, a software engineer with more than a decade of experience across mobile apps, API integrations, testing and delivery. My foundation is Android and Huawei ecosystems, including fintech.

My focus now is applied AI engineering: LLM-powered applications connected to real workflows. Through Clementine and Sharmaine I work on local inference, retrieval, context management, orchestration and API-based model integration, including Amazon Bedrock. I have also worked with Dockerized apps and AWS container workflows (ECR, EC2).

I care about the engineering around the model: how an app picks context, routes a task, validates output, controls tool access and hands a human the right point to review. I use AI-assisted coding to move faster, then inspect, test and verify what ships.
